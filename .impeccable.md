## Design Context

### Users
SlopSkip is for people who spend time in short-form feeds and want local, user-controlled filtering for low-effort AI content. They are likely adjusting the extension while TikTok or Instagram Reels is open, so the interface should support quick decisions, cautious tuning, and easy recovery from mistakes.

### Brand Personality
Calm, local-first, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a precise control room for a personal feed filter, not a flashy AI detector or a judgmental moderation tool.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained utility-dashboard style with warm neutrals, grounded green accents, crisp dividers, compact controls, and clear state feedback. Avoid neon cyberpunk, generic glass cards, heavy gradients, and marketing-style hero layouts. The interface should feel practical, private, and explainable.

### Design Principles
- Make tuning feel safe by showing what changed, what is saved, and how strict each setting is.
- Prefer dense but readable controls over decorative panels.
- Keep privacy and locality visible through plain UI affordances, not long explanatory copy.
- Use strong focus states, accessible touch targets, and resilient layouts for extension windows.
- Design for false positives and false negatives by making undo, reset, import, and export paths obvious.
